SCIENCE NOTES

Sources & useful distinctions

Water distribution estimates are approximate. Every percentage on this site names its reference total. The familiar 97% / 3% split is a classroom rounding; more detailed estimates use about 97.5% saline water and 2.5% freshwater.

Residence times are approximate averages or broad teaching ranges. They vary between locations and are not deadlines for individual molecules. The long groundwater range refers to deeper, slower systems; shallow groundwater can move much faster.

  • NOAA: the water cycle and water distribution
  • USGS: groundwater flow
  • USGS: ice, snow and glaciers
  • USGS: water budgets and residence times
