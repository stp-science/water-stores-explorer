# Year 9 Spheres of the Earth revision

Student revision for the supplied LP1–5 teaching sequence: spheres and interactions, water movement, solar heating, clouds and mountain rainfall, water stores and timescales.

The page includes six revision sections, 24 multiple-choice questions with explanatory feedback, a balanced 12-question mixed quiz, six explanation prompts with success criteria, and a printable 30-mark practice paper. Questions use new wording, contexts and data rather than reproducing the topic assessment.

Serve this directory as a static webpage. No build, external fonts, tracking or account is required. Topic confidence and best quiz scores are stored in the browser on the current device. Written explanation attempts are temporary and clear when leaving that activity.

The student paper is available from the page. The teacher answer guide is supplied separately.
